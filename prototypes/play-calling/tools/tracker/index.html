How these numbers are made

Rosters: weeks 1–8 of 2025-26, each auto-drafted four ways (balance, offense, defense, flexibility), with suggested heroes and Smart-picked games: 32 games per bot and difficulty.

Smart bot: scripts/books.ts with BOOKS set to the plan. Expert bot: scripts/sim.ts with ROSTERS=8 and STRATEGY set to the plan. The plans were picked from a search of every four-play book that uses all five heroes.

Status: Works means ahead on Normal and Hard for both bots; Thin on Hard means the smart bot's Hard margin is under +6; Expert only means only the bot that searches defenses wins with it.

Difficulty (Nov 28): the horde scores more instead of Champions getting more HP. The ram or Ritual scores +1 each turn it scores, and on Hard a Champion’s hit scores 3. Champion HP is ×1.25 on both difficulties.

The Crypt (Nov 28): the Necromancer’s ritual scores 2 a turn until halftime; his skeletons ward him while 3 or more stand; take him down to break it (he rises as a Wraith while the phylactery stands), or face the Lich after halftime.

Defense (Nov 28): Defense is a count: each hero off the ball adds to a capped chance of stopping the horde’s play; the Rogue can Steal from a Slowed or Exposed Champion; Spacing passes Quick; a stop gives the next finisher Crit.
